# 🧾 Result Evaluation Rubric Tool

This rubric evaluates the **quality, usefulness, and alignment of outputs** generated by Large Language Models (LLMs). It complements the Prompt Evaluation Rubric by focusing on **response-level performance**, ensuring high-quality feedback, factual accuracy, and goal alignment.

---

## 🎯 Rubric Goals

- Ensure **output quality and reliability** across use cases (e.g., coding, tutoring, summarization, etc.)
- Support both **manual evaluation** and **automated scoring pipelines**
- Provide **feedback-based diagnostics** for response improvement

---

## 🧩 Evaluation Dimensions

Each generated result is scored across **five key dimensions**, each rated from **1 (poor)** to **5 (excellent)**.

| Dimension | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| 1. Factual Accuracy | Checks correctness, logic, and absence of hallucinations |
| 2. Instructional Alignment | Measures how well the output follows the given prompt or task |
| 3. Coherence & Structure | Evaluates clarity, flow, and completeness |
| 4. Insight & Usefulness | Assesses depth, reasoning, and actionable value |
| 5. Tone & Style | Checks appropriateness, audience fit, and engagement level |

---

## 🔍 Dimension Details

### 1. Factual Accuracy

| Score | Definition | Guiding Questions | Examples |
|-------|------------|-------------------|----------|
| 5 | Fully accurate and logically consistent | Is every key detail verifiable and precise? | Correct historical facts, working code |
| 4 | Mostly accurate, minor issues | Any small inaccuracies that don’t change the meaning? | Typo in date, but conclusion still valid |
| 3 | Some factual gaps or shallow logic | Are key assumptions flawed or unsupported? | Incomplete code or partially wrong math |
| 2 | Major inaccuracies or unsupported claims | Are there glaring errors? | Incorrect scientific facts |
| 1 | Hallucinated or incoherent | Does it invent or mislead completely? | Cites fake references or fails entirely |

---

### 2. Instructional Alignment

| Score | Definition | Guiding Questions | Examples |
|-------|------------|-------------------|----------|
| 5 | Fully follows the task as asked | Does it respond directly and completely? | Summarizes as requested with no added info |
| 4 | Mostly aligned, some extra or missing parts | Does it slightly go off-track or miss a part? | Summary + analysis when only summary was asked |
| 3 | Partially follows the instruction | Does it do only one part of a multi-step task? | Lists facts but fails to compare them |
| 2 | Poorly aligned or misunderstood | Is the answer unrelated or inverted? | Asked to critique but describes instead |
| 1 | Totally ignores or misunderstands task | Did it answer something else? | Responds with joke or irrelevant content |

---

### 3. Coherence & Structure

| Score | Definition | Guiding Questions | Examples |
|-------|------------|-------------------|----------|
| 5 | Very clear, logically structured, no errors | Does it flow smoothly and logically? | Bullet points for steps, good intro/conclusion |
| 4 | Mostly clear, minor gaps or awkward phrasing | Are transitions slightly rough? | Repetition or abrupt section breaks |
| 3 | Some disorganization or clarity issues | Is meaning hard to follow in places? | Redundant phrases or inconsistent formatting |
| 2 | Difficult to follow | Is it scattered or lacking structure? | Jumps between unrelated ideas |
| 1 | Confusing or unreadable | Is it almost gibberish? | Run-on sentences or mixed language |

---

### 4. Insight & Usefulness

| Score | Definition | Guiding Questions | Examples |
|-------|------------|-------------------|----------|
| 5 | Offers original insight, deep reasoning, and value | Would a human expert be impressed? | Nuanced tradeoffs in tech design |
| 4 | Clear reasoning and good value | Is it thoughtful and useful? | Good interpretation of trends or case studies |
| 3 | Some insight but limited depth | Is it surface-level or generic? | Lists obvious pros/cons |
| 2 | Minimal value or shallow ideas | Is it just filler or keyword spam? | Repeats what prompt already asked |
| 1 | No insight, unhelpful | Is it just noise? | “I don’t know” or vague rambling |

---

### 5. Tone & Style

| Score | Definition | Guiding Questions | Examples |
|-------|------------|-------------------|----------|
| 5 | Fully appropriate tone for the context | Is it engaging, professional, or empathetic as needed? | Friendly for tutor, formal for report |
| 4 | Mostly good, minor mismatches | Is tone fine but inconsistent? | Too casual for academic topic |
| 3 | Mixed tone, awkward shifts | Does it change style oddly? | Jokes in a serious task |
| 2 | Poor tone fit | Does tone create misunderstanding? | Sarcasm in a help request |
| 1 | Inappropriate or offensive | Does tone cause discomfort? | Rude, irrelevant, or hostile language |
